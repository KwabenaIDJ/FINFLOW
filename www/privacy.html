1. Information We Collect

FinFlow collects user-entered financial transactions, budget limits, savings goals, and account profile preferences necessary to compute personal dashboard analytics.

2. Data Storage & Security

Your financial ledger data is stored locally on your device and synchronized over encrypted SSL connections to Supabase PostgreSQL database servers. FinFlow uses end-to-end HTTPS encryption for all network requests.

3. Zero Third-Party Data Selling

FinFlow respects user privacy. We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal transaction data, email addresses, or financial records with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Payment Processing Security

All subscription payments are securely processed directly by Paystack PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. FinFlow never stores or handles your debit card numbers, Mobile Money PINs, or bank credentials.

For questions regarding your privacy or data, contact us at finflow64@gmail.com.
