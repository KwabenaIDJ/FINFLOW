1. Service Description

FinFlow is a cloud-based and local-first personal financial management, budgeting, and analytics software platform. FinFlow provides users with tools for recording personal ledger transactions, tracking spending limits, calculating savings goals, and accessing automated money organizational insights.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER & SCOPE OF ADVICE: FinFlow provides self-directed personal budgeting tools, educational financial guides, and automated money organizational insights (including AI Money Coach responses). FinFlow does NOT provide licensed fiduciary advice, certified financial planning, regulated investment management, asset custody, or money transfer services. All automated AI insights and literacy materials are intended strictly for educational self-tracking and informational organization.

2. Subscriptions & Pricing Model

Basic financial literacy guides, transaction logging, and budgeting tools are provided 100% free of charge. Premium Membership features (including FinFlow AI Coach analytics, custom themes, and unlimited PDF exports) are available for $1.99 USD / month (or equivalent local currency automatically converted in real-time at live market exchange rates via Paystack).

3. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of data entered into their personal ledger and keeping their private account login credentials confidential.

4. Data Ownership & Storage

Your financial ledger data is stored locally on your device and synchronized over encrypted SSL connections to Supabase PostgreSQL database servers. FinFlow uses end-to-end HTTPS encryption for all network requests.
