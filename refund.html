Refund & Cancellation Policy

CUSTOMER SATISFACTION COMMITMENT: We want you to be 100% satisfied with FinFlow whether you subscribe on the web or through our Android mobile application. Please review the refund and cancellation procedures below tailored for your platform.

1. Web Platform Purchases (Paystack & Mobile Money)

For subscriptions and purchases initiated directly on the FinFlow web dashboard (via Paystack, Mobile Money MTN/Telecel/AT, or bank cards):

  • 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee: If you are not completely satisfied with your Premium features within 7 days of your purchase on the web, you are entitled to a full 100% refund.
  • How to Request: Email our support desk at finflow64@gmail.com with your registered username or Paystack transaction reference.
  • Processing Time: Approved web refunds are credited back to your original Mobile Money wallet or bank card within 24 to 48 business hours via Paystack.

2. Android App Purchases (Google Play In-App Billing)

For subscriptions and purchases initiated inside the FinFlow Android application, payments are securely processed by Google Play In-App Billing and are governed by Google Play's Refund Policies:

  • Within 48 Hours: You can request a refund directly from Google Play by visiting your Google Play Order History at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory or through the Google Play Store app (Account > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history).
  • After 48 Hours / Developer Assistance: If you encounter any technical issues or need help, email our support team at finflow64@gmail.com with your Google Play Order Number (beginning with GPA., found in your Google Play receipt email). We will review and process eligible refunds through the Google Play Developer Console in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

3. Subscription Cancellation & Auto-Renewal

You may cancel your Premium subscription at any time without any cancellation fees:

  • Google Play Subscriptions (Android): Open the Google Play Store app > tap your Profile icon > select Payments & subscriptions > tap Subscriptions > select FinFlow > tap Cancel subscription. Your premium benefits will remain active until the end of your prepaid billing period.
  • Web Subscriptions: You can turn off auto-renewal or delete your account in the FinFlow Settings tab, or email finflow64@gmail.com for immediate cancellation assistance.

4. Customer Support

For any billing questions, refund inquiries, or account assistance, please contact our dedicated support team at finflow64@gmail.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.
